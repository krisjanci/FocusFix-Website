Privacy

FocusFix is a personal macOS app in development. It is separate from its developer’s N-of-1 study. The current website download is signed and Apple notarized; see the installation guide.

Recording on your Mac

Recording requires your choice and macOS Input Monitoring permission. FocusFix saves keyboard timing and delete-key events, clicks, scrolling, app changes, one-second mouse movement summaries, breaks, reminders, and labels you give to work and non-work periods. Automatic recording can include non-work activity. It does not save typed words, individual letter keys, app names, window titles, URLs, screenshots, or exact cursor paths. Detailed timing can still identify or reveal sensitive habits.

Start work begins a manual recording or marks work during automatic recording. End work stops manual recording or returns automatic recording to Unknown. Pause recording and Quit stop capture. A closed window may leave the menu-bar app running. Idle time, sleep, lock, or loss of permission also stop capture. The app shows its current recording state. A reminder’s “I’m not working” action changes the label from that point forward; earlier periods can be corrected in Sessions.

Recordings stay in the app’s private folder on your Mac unless you explicitly enable the separate sharing option below. You can review, export, or delete sessions. Deleting a local session moves it to Trash; you must empty Trash to remove that copy. Your exports and backups are separate copies. FocusFix does not separately encrypt local recordings.

Optional account and groups

You can use local features as a guest. If you create an account, Supabase receives your email, authentication information, and account identifier. A profile name and photo are sent only when you save them to your account. If you join a private group, other members can see your chosen name, standard avatar, and shared scores. Signing in does not enable recording sharing.

Optional sharing with the developer

Detailed recording uploads are disabled in the current website build while the service is being prepared.

The developer may approve specific signed-in accounts and turn collection on or off in Supabase. Even if approved, you must separately choose to share in FocusFix. Only completed recordings started after that consent are eligible. FocusFix sends detailed activity archives to a private Supabase storage bucket for individual product development. The archive includes event timing, mouse movement summaries, work and break context, reminders, and session metadata. It excludes typed text, account credentials, profile name and photo, and study surveys. Your Supabase account can be linked to the archive; it is not anonymous. The developer may inspect or download it.

You can turn sharing off in Settings → Privacy & Data Sharing. This cancels pending uploads on your Mac and sends a withdrawal to Supabase when connected. An offline withdrawal cannot reach the server until you reconnect and sign in; the app blocks further uploads from that installation meanwhile. Previously uploaded files remain until you request deletion. Collection stops when the developer switches it off, revokes your approval, or the per-account archive limit is reached.

Deletion and retention

Uploaded recordings are kept until you request deletion. Use Delete shared data in Settings → Privacy & Data Sharing to stop uploads and delete hosted recordings. Account deletion also requests this. The developer must separately remove any downloaded copies and handle service backups; deletion of those copies is not automatic. Minimal consent and deletion records may remain to document the request. Deleting shared data does not delete local sessions, exports, or your own backups. You can request help through the support link below without posting personal data publicly.

Website and limitations

This GitHub Pages website has no analytics scripts, cookies, or forms. GitHub may process ordinary request logs when serving it. FocusFix is an experimental activity tool, not a medical device or a validated measure of productivity or fatigue.

For help, use FocusFix support on GitHub. Issues are public; do not post recordings, credentials, or personal information. The app source is available under GPL-3.0-only.
